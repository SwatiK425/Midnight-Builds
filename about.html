the human

Hi, I'm Swati.

I build things for the problems I can't stop thinking about.

I'm a builder who likes to solve everyday, real problems. I have a day job in tech, so most of this happens at night with a cup of coffee, after the house goes quiet.

One uncomfortable question kept coming back to me: could a stranger read my users' data? Finding out took more effort than it should have, so I made MakerGuard. Now anyone building on Supabase can find out in about a minute.

Some problems start at home. I wanted something small to do with my little one that didn't come with a chart, a streak, or a side of guilt. Cuddles & Cue gives you one three-minute idea a day, using whatever is already in your home.

Others start with curiosity. I understand things best once I've tried to build them. The DLP Guardrail and SOC Copilot started that way, with me wanting to know how AI leaks data and how security teams survive the flood of alerts. Some of it is still unfinished, and I label it that way.

If you're stuck on a problem that won't leave you alone, tell me about it. That's usually where the next thing starts.
